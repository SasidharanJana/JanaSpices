“If it isn't good enough for our own kitchen, it doesn't leave our warehouse.”

Grown in the Ghats. Graded by hand.

Our pepper comes from smallholder farms along the Kerala–Tamil Nadu hills, where monsoon rain and high-altitude shade give the berries their heat and fragrance. Every lot is cleaned, dried to a controlled moisture level and checked before it's packed.

Traceable origin

Food-grade packs for retail; lined bags for wholesale lots.
